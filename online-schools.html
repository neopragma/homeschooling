Online Schools and Lessons

Many homeschoolers either cannot or do not wish to try and handle every lesson, every subject, and every learning obstacle that comes up in the course of educating their children. A number of accredited schools offer remote lessons, either with a live teacher or in the form of videos and supplementary materials.

It is possible to have your child attend school entirely with one of these services, or to use one or more services as a supplement to your homeschooling program. Some offer a complete program through high school and others offer various forms of study help and supplemental lessons.

Most of these are commercial offerings, not free.
