Developmental Stages

As a parent, you already know children pass through a number of stages in their development. Most websites that describe developmental stages focus on birth through about 5 years of age, as development is rapid and changes are dramatic during that phase of life. For purposes of homeschooling, developmental stages between the ages of about 6 and 18 are of more interest.

Different researchers and psychologists recognize slightly different age levels and characteristics for each stage of human development. If you can get an approximate idea of what to expect from your children, you can craft your teaching approach and homeschooling materials appropriately for each age group or level of maturity. Combined with your close familiarity with your children, this will help you create lesson plans that work well for them.
