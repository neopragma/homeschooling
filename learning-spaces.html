Learning Spaces

Ideas for organizing areas in your home to support homeschooling.

General

The design of the physical spaces you use for homeschooling will depend on a handful of key factors:

  • the space available in your home;
  • your budget for setting up the area(s);
  • whether the space can be dedicated to homeschooling or must be a multi-purpose space;
  • the teaching approach you plan to use; and
  • the ages of the children.

Online resources

There is no "best" way to organize the physical space for homeschooling. Don't worry excessively about making the space "perfect." Many homeschoolers have published their own solutions online. As you can see, there are many variations and practical adaptations. They all "work."
