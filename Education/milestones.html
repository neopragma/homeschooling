  • Understands the appropriate balance between individual liberty and civic responsibility

  • Has learned at least one marketable skill with which to support self

3.1. Appreciation for the Human Experience blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah blah
  • Appreciates the breadth of human experience including literature, music, fine arts, popular arts, sports and games, and similar
  • Is familiar with and accepting of various cultures and religions
3.2. Appreciation for Science
  • Not applicable
  • May have begun to show interest in how things work and how the world came to be
  • Has been exposed to a range of scientific information presented at an age-appropriate level
  • May have taken an interest in a science-related hobby such as astronomy, geology, or robots
  • Has learned many facts about nature and science
  • Can do the experiments in Cara Ocobock's "Science Experiment Laboratory Manual", or work at a similar level.
  • Can explain the basic ideas of the scientific method
  • Can distinguish fantasy from reality, magical thinking from scientific thinking
  • Can apply the scientific method to investigations of events and items in the world in a basic way
  • Is developing a sense for which sources are credible and which are not
  • Understands scientific principles and the scientific method
  • Generally accepts conclusions from apparently-credible sources
  • Understands scientific principles and the scientific method
  • Has an understanding of basic statistics and how statistical information can be used and abused
  • Respects the knowledge and integrity of credible scientists and research results
