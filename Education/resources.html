State of Arizona

The general rules are defined at the State level, but the Affadavit of Intent to Homeschool and certain other paperwork and regulations are handled at the County level. Consult the website of your County government for details, or this list of County Education Service Agencies on the Arizona Department of Education website.

  • Arizona Revised Statute 15-802: School instruction; exceptions; violations; classification; definitions
  • Arizona Revised Statute 15-745: Homeschooled children; testing; prohibition
  • Arizona Revised Statute 15-2404: State control over nonpublic schools; prohibition; application
  • Arizona Revised Statute 15-2402: Arizona empowerment scholarship accounts; funds
  • Arizona Revised Statute 15-803. School attendance; exemptions; definitions
  • AZ Dept. of Education K-12 Standards
