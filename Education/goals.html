Goal of education

To prepare a person to be a functioning, contributing member of a diverse and democratic society.

Fundamental premise

To achieve the goal of education, a person needs to develop in each of the following three aspects.

Aspect 1: Self

A functioning adult...

  • 1.1. Is able to think critically, analytically, synthetically, and logically. Is able to shift perspective between holistic or big-picture thinking and compartmentalized or detail-level thinking as appropriate. Distinguishes between obvious nonsense and potentially-credible information. Knows how to follow up to determine the validity of information.
  • 1.2. Knows how to deal with everyday activities and tasks - self-care (health and fitness, cooking, treating minor injuries, mending clothing, getting the right amount of sleep, handling stress, recognizing the signs of addiction), interacting effectively with others (co-workers, neighbors, family members, people encountered in everyday situations, law enforcement), basic personal and household accounting (budgeting, paying rent, completing tax returns, financial planning, purchasing property, investing, recognizing scams), using common infrastructure and typical personal devices (public transit, automobile, phone, computer), basic self-defense (situational awareness, de-escalation and negotiation skills, basic martial arts, handling typical weapons), taking care of the home (cleaning, painting, handyman-level repairs, routine maintenance), etc.
  • 1.3. Takes responsibility for his/her own actions - see Christopher Avery's Responsibility Process.
  • 1.4. Is self-aware: (a) understands how inherent bias arising from cultural upbringing may affect one's perspective, as well as how cognitive biases may affect one's reasoning; (b) understands own tendencies and general traits (e.g., introversion/extroversion, visual/verbal/kinesthetic learner, etc.) and how to operate effectively alone and in collaboration with others by managing these tendencies.
  • 1.5. Is able to control his/her own emotional reactions to unexpected events and problems to the extent that he/she has a fair chance of responding to any situation in a way that leads to a positive outcome or, at least, to minimal damage.
  • 1.6. Is able to delay gratification when necessary to achieve larger goals or greater benefits in the long term.
  • 1.7. Is able to accept the differences of others and to recognize the rights of others to live as they please, within the general constraints of law and culture.

Aspect 2: Intersection of self and society

A functioning adult...

  • 2.1. Understands the relationship of the individual to the society of which he/she is a part and understands the appropriate balance between individual liberty and civic obligation, as appropriate to the general culture of the society.
  • 2.2. Possesses one or more marketable skills and understands general expectations of employees (showing up on time, acting ethically, etc.).
  • 2.3. Understands and participates in the democratic process. Understands the purpose, history, strucrture, and operation of government. Knows how to find information about candidates and political parties. Knows how to register to vote and cast a vote. Understands the basic philosophy of common political systems or models.
  • 2.4. Lives and acts according to an internal moral code that he/she can articulate clearly. The personal moral code is compatible with the general mores of the society.

Aspect 3: Intersection of society and the world

A functioning adult...

  • 3.1. Appreciates the breadth of human experience - literature, popular arts, fine arts, sports, religion, culture. In-depth learning or direct practice in these areas is not necessary but should be available based on personal interest.
  • 3.2. Has a general understanding of scientific advancements and technology. In-depth learning is not necessary but should be available based on personal interest.
  • 3.3. Has a general understanding of history and how the past has influenced the present. In-depth learning is not necessary but should be available based on personal interest.
