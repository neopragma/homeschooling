Free Learning Resources - Life Skills - Advertising & Propaganda

This is a broad topic, and when it comes to contemporary issues and conflicts, any source of information is likely to be biased in some way. Let the reader beware! These resources cover a blend of political, religious, and commercial propaganda and advertising, both historical and current. Some general topic areas are broken out, but the links are not organized in detail around these various issues. Many of the references are themselves sources of propaganda. Homeschoolers are advised to review material carefully before incorporating it into lessons.

Education about Propaganda and Misinformation

Some countries have instituted educational programs or curriculum content to help young people learn how to spot scams and misinformation and how to cope with it effectively. In most cases there isn't much online material that homeschoolers can download to help them incorporate this subject into their curricula, but Finland and Sweden seem to have the most information available.
