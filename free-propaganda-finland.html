Free Learning Resources - Life Skills - Advertising & Propaganda - Finland's Approach to Education on Misinformation and Propaganda

Dave Nicolette

The Finnish education system is widely praised for including education about misinformation, propaganda, and online scams. Homeschoolers may find useful ideas and teaching materials from Finnish sources.

Scam awareness and critical thinking are baked into the civics curriculum in Finnish schools. It is not a separate subject. They consider it part of digital literacy, an important life skill for anyone living in the 21st century. They also hold contests in which students prepare misinformation, disinformation, and malinformation and challenge each other to recognize and analyze it.

Definitions

The Finnish program distinguishes these terms:

  • Misinformation. False or inaccurate information shared without any intent to deceive. Errors or misstatements may not be deliberate. Social media posts of a political nature often fall into this category.
  • Disinformation. Deliberately deceptive or manipulative information. Propaganda generally falls into this category.
  • Malinformation. Genuine information that is used in a misleading way, for instance by omitting contextual information or counterbalancing facts. This is very common and runs the gamut from commercial advertising to simple gossip.

The Finnish Five Questions

Finnish students learn to apply these questions to any advertisement or social media post:

  1. Who created it?
  2. What does the creator want me to think, feel, or do?
  3. What evidence is provided?
  4. What information is missing?
  5. Who benefits if I believe or share it?

Avoid a simplistic true/false assessment. Instead, ask what we can establish and what remains unverified.

The SIFT Process

This is a series of steps students can practice to help identify inaccurate information.

  1. Stop before reacting or sharing.
  2. Investigate the source.
  3. Find independent reporting or expert evidence.
  4. Trace the claim to its original source.
