Free Learning Resources - Humanities - History

Not all these resources are free. This indication ($$$) means the resources are for sale or that some form of subscription or membership is required to access some or all content on the site. For-profit publishers often offer some content free of charge, either as samples or under the terms of a trial subscription.

Video entries marked (AC) are narrated tours done in Assassins' Creed Odyssey discovery mode, which provides a historically-accurate 3D rendering of certain ancient sites. Each video features a guest historian who provides background information and context for what the viewer is seeing.

  • General or Multi-Subject Resources
    • WorldHistory.org
      Numerous resources including articles, audio, video, maps, photos, and lesson packs.
    • Lesson Planet: History Teacher Resources
      This site has a smattering of lesson plans, developed independently of each other, that touch on various historical periods and locales and are aimed at different age groups; not a comprehensive set of lesson plans to guide students through a curriculum.
    • Education.com: History lesson plans
      This site has a smattering of lesson plans, developed independently of each other, that touch on various historical periods and locales and are aimed at different age groups; not a comprehensive set of lesson plans to guide students through a curriculum.
    • British Museum
      The British Museum has learning resources for school-age children and adults, some online and some designed for guided in-person visits to the museum. The school-oriented material is geared for the UK educational system and is organized by Key Stages. Well organized and authoritative but not comprehensive.

      Searchable historical maps with a timeline slider. Goes back to 1826. No street view.
