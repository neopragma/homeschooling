Online Resources

Online courses, general information, online resources for teachers and homeschoolers by subject.

We've tried to find genuinely free resources, but in nearly all cases there's a "catch." Of course, everyone has to earn a living, and providing information on the Internet has a cost; but watch out for "tricks." If a site claims to offer free lessons or materials, but requires you to download a piece of software to access them, that's a red flag. You don't know whether malware will be installed along with that software. If a site claims to offer free resources but requires "sign up" to access them, proceed with caution. It's up to you whether to submit your personal information to the site to register for their services. It might be fine, or it might be problematic.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) services can be very useful for locating information on the Internet. Some are available free of charge and others require a paid subscription. These tools can scour the Internet very rapidly and provide you with a summary of relevant results. But they do not know how to "vet" the information they find, so you must review whatever they present to you.
