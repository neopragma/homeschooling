Non-free Music Learning Resources

Online, downloadable, printable, physical learning resources not free of charge.

General

  • Coursera
    Coursera is a portal for online training courses in a wide range of subjects. The courses available vary over time as people add new material. In the general area of "music" the courses range from music theory to how-to-play-the-[insert instrument here] to courses on music production, songwriting, and the music business.
  • Make Music
    This is a resource for music educators and students. It offers various printable items and practice materials for sight-reading and etudes. More interesting is the interactive feature that lets the user choose their part from an orchestral or band piece and play along with a synthesized performance of the piece. Beware of the pricing shown on the website, as it includes a bulk purchase discount that will not apply to a single individual subscription.
