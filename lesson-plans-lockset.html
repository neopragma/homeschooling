Sample Lesson Plan for "Install Lockset on Interior Door"

Item Content
Grade Level(s) 7+
Learning Objectives Learn how to install a lockset on a standard US hollow-core interior door.
Prerequisites Basic skills and safety for hand tools and power tools.
Duration Simple version: 10 minutes
Advanced version: 1 hour
Vocabulary Vocabulary to be introduced in context in the course of the work:
  • back-set
  • bevel
  • chisel
  • drill guide
  • edge bore hole
  • faceplate
  • hole saw
  • hollow-core
  • jamb
  • latch
  • latch hole
  • latch plate
  • lockset
  • mortise
  • mortise jig (usually included with lockset kit)
  • router bit (usually included with lockset kit or drill bit set)
  • strike plate (usually included with lockset kit)
00:05. Simple version: Remove the Old Lockset Use a screwdriver to remove the screws that hold the lockset in place on the door and remove the parts.
00:05. Simple version: Re-install the Same Lockset Insert the parts in the reverse order from when you removed them, and secure with screws.
00:45. Advanced version: Prepare the New Door
  • Watch the video one or more times before beginning the actual work.
  • Measure from the top of the old door to its latch hole. Mark that position on the new door.
  • Lay the door horizontally across two (2) sawhorses.
  • Select the back-set on the drill guide.
  • Align the drill guide on the side of the door according to your measurement, and secure it with screws.
  • Put on your safety gear.
  • Using a power drill with a 2 3/8" hole saw, drill the face bore hole for the new lockset, stopping halfway through to avoid creating splinters.
  • Turn the door over and drill through the rest of the way from the other side.
  • Drill the edge bore hole for the latch using a 1" hole saw.
  • Remove the drill guide from the door.
  • Screw the mortise jig onto the side of the door over the latch hole.
  • Using a router bit of the correct length, drill into the door following the channels in the template.
  • Use a chisel to clean out the rest of the material down to the depth that was set by the router bit.
  • Hang the door (instructions not included with this lesson plan).
00:15. Advanced version: Install the New Lockset
  • You won't need the eye and ear protection from this point forward.
  • Install the new strikeplate on the jamb using screws.
  • Push the latch assembly through the latch hole with the beveled side facing the jamb. If it doesn't slide in easily into the mortise, you can tap it into place carefully with a small block of wood between the latch and the hammer or mallet.
  • Screw the latch plate into place.
  • Insert the outside lever into the hole in the latch.
  • Align the inside lever with the outside lever and secure with screws.
Preparation
  • Simple version: Remove and re-install the same lockset on an existing door
    • Phillips Head screwdriver, also known as cross-tip screwdriver, to fit the screws that hold the existing lockset in place.
    • Tape measure
    • Pencil
  • Advanced version: Install a lockset on a new hollow-core door
    • All the items needed for the simple version
    • Two (2) sawhorses
    • Chisel
    • Drill guide
    • Ear protection
    • Eye protection
    • Hammer or rubber mallet
    • Power drill
    • Router bit
    • Wood block
    • Work gloves
  • Cue up video:
    • How to Install a Lockset (Lowe's Home Improvement)
    • Corresponding written instructions
