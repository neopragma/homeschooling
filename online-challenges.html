Online Challenges

This page lists websites that have one or more puzzles or challenges or projects that can be useful for homeschoolers.

Sites are listed in alphabetical order by name. They are not broken out by age group, genre, learning goals, or subject matter.

25 Logic Puzzles

Ages: 8 and up

URL: https://parade.com/970343/parade/logic-puzzles/

Short "word problems" at varying levels of difficulty. Answers are provided.

  • Logical thinking
  • Problem-solving

Aha! Logic Puzzles

Ages: 8 and up

URL: https://www.ahapuzzles.com/logic/logic-puzzles/

The site explains how to use a grid (a truth table) to solve "word problems" and has a quite a lot of problems at different difficulty levels.

  • Logical thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Using the grid method to solve word problems

Brilliant ($$$)

Ages: 10 and up

URL: https://brilliant.org/

Hands-on, interactive problem-solving designed to introduce math and science topics and strengthen skills. This is not a free resource. There are a few practice problems you can access without signing up at https://brilliant.org/logic/?subtopic=puzzles. Subject areas include the following.

  • Algebra
  • Geometry
  • Number Theory
  • Calculus
  • Probability
  • Basic Mathematics
  • Classical Mechanics
  • Electricity and Magnetism
  • Computer Science
  • Quantitative Finance
  • Chemistry

Logic Puzzles

Ages: 10 and up

URL: http://www.folj.com/puzzles/

Logical word problems with solutions.

  • Logical thinking
  • Problem-solving

Project Euler

Ages: 14 and up

URL: https://projecteuler.net/

"Project Euler is a series of challenging mathematical/computer programming problems that will require more than just mathematical insights to solve. [...] The intended audience include students for whom the basic curriculum is not feeding their hunger to learn, adults whose background was not primarily mathematics but had an interest in things mathematical, and professionals who want to keep their problem solving and mathematics on the cutting edge."

  • Inductive reasoning
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Problem-solving
  • Patience

Puzzle Baron's Logic Puzzles

Ages: 8 and up

URL: https://logic.puzzlebaron.com/

Logical word problems to be solved using the grid (truth table) method. Registration is required. The site is ad-supported and they have a paid option to eliminate the ads.

  • Logical thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Using the grid method to solve word problems

Riddles Logic Puzzles

Ages: 8 and up

URL: https://www.riddles.com/logic-puzzles

Word problems with solutions. Basic.

  • Logical thinking
  • Problem-solving
